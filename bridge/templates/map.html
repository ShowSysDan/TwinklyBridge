The bridge uses its own certificate, so the phone warns the first time. On iPhone: Show Details, then visit this website, then Visit Website.

1. What to map

Darken the room if you can. Put the phone on a tripod or lean it on something: it must not move during the run (about 20 s). Every LED you want mapped has to be in the picture. Mapping several controllers together puts them in one shared picture.

Frame the whole installation, then hold still.
